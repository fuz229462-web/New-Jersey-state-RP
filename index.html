Community Rules

  1. Value Your Life (FearRP): You must value your character's life in life-threatening scenarios.
  2. No Random Deathmatch (RDM): Attacking or killing players without valid roleplay context is prohibited.
  3. Stay in Character: Maintain RP at all times while in-game. Use OOC chat only when necessary.
  4. Respect Community Members: Harassment, hate speech, or toxicity will result in an immediate ban.
