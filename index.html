What We're About

Serious roleplay in Emergency Response: Liberty County.

Realistic Roleplay

Every scene is treated like it matters. No fail RP, no minge behaviour, no breaking character for a laugh.

Immersive Law Enforcement

Proper traffic stops, pursuits handled by policy, radio discipline, and callsigns used the way real units would.

Structured Departments

Clear ranks, training before you go solo, and supervisors who actually run the shift.

Active Staff

Scheduled Sessions

Growing Community

A small, tight-knit group where new members get trained properly rather than thrown straight into a patrol car.

Community Rules

Full ruleset is pinned in the Discord. These are the ones that get people removed.

  1. Value Your Life (FearRP): Act like your character wants to survive. Don't run at an armed officer.
  2. No Random Deathmatch (RDM): Killing or ramming without roleplay context is an instant removal.
  3. Stay in Character: Keep RP going in-session. Use OOC channels for anything out of character.
  4. Follow Department Policy: Pursuits, use of force, and callsigns are done by the book, not improvised.
  5. Respect Everyone: Harassment, slurs, or toxicity results in an immediate permanent ban.
  6. Listen to Staff: Staff decisions are final in-session. Appeal in Discord, not in chat.
